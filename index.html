I'm Khushbu Sonkusare — a video editor with 2+ years of experience creating short-form videos, Reels, YouTube Shorts, and social media content. My journey began with a fascination for how powerful editing can transform a simple clip into an emotional experience.

From clean cuts and pacing to typography, transitions, sound design, color grading, and After Effects animation — I focus on turning raw footage into engaging, polished content.

I focus on strong hooks, visual storytelling, music synchronization, captions, and polished motion to create content that feels engaging from the first frame to the last.
